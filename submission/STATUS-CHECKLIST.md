## Deterministic trend rule for review

Eligible BLS evidence must have a retrieval/check within 30 days, publication within 550 days, a projection horizon including the current year, and no stale/fetch-failure flag. Positive Data Scientists projected growth puts the related Data Analyst exploration first, negative growth puts it later. Zero, missing or ineligible evidence removes this adjustment. Other roles remain direction unknown. Personal alignment scores keep their meaning. Positive growth prioritizes statistical reasoning among ready Foundation steps as an editorial practice decision, not a measured skill-demand statistic. Prerequisites always govern. U.S. related-role evidence has low transfer confidence for Malaysian decisions. News does not change scores or demand judgments. Final source of truth: `docs/TREND-METHODOLOGY.md` and the reviewed code.
