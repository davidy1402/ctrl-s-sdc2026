# Wireframes

Save the actual visual wireframe exports or a viewable design link in the Google Design folder. The text summary from the design AI is not the visual artifact itself.

Current discussion flow: About You, Explore Your Options, Your Next Steps. The team has not approved the final audience or product scope.

Review together:

- Is the next action easy to understand?
- Can people enter their real learning-time constraints instead of receiving a stereotype-based default?
- Are scenarios labelled as assumptions, with an explanation of what changes?
- Do role and skill recommendations have places for sources and meaningful SDG explanations?
- Are sample scores clearly distinct from hiring probabilities?
- Are teammate ownership notes kept outside the user interface?

Keep prototype sample content distinct from verified facts and working features.
