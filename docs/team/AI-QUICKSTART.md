# A short AI practice session

Use tools you can already access. The team has not bought a subscription or selected an app model/provider as part of setup. A coding assistant subscription and the running application's AI/API bill are separate costs.

## Twenty-minute introduction

1. Five minutes: read the Domain 1 challenge and explain the intended user in your own words.
2. Five minutes: give an AI assistant one small task with the relevant source text and a clear output.
3. Five minutes: inspect the answer. Check facts against the source; run code changes if applicable.
4. Five minutes: explain what changed, save the result link and update the tracker.

## Examples

- Rania: turn team-approved notes into a short proposal outline without adding claims or results.
- Anes: compare a proposed SDG connection with the exact source definition and identify unsupported reasoning.
- Sushi: create a course-check checklist, then personally open each original course page and record the title, cost, duration and checked date.
- David and Sodik: ask a coding assistant to explain the relevant module, propose one small change, implement it within scope and report what was actually checked.

Do not ask separate sessions to rewrite the whole app independently. Name the files/scope and coordinate who owns overlapping changes. Do not paste secrets or real CVs. Keep a record of actual AI tools/models used, how they helped and how the team reviewed their outputs.

## Before paying

Identify the exact blocked task, check existing account access and official sponsor resources, agree a spending cap, and choose one necessary purchase only if the free/existing route cannot support the task. Do not share passwords or bypass account terms. No purchase or paid resource activation is authorised by this document.
